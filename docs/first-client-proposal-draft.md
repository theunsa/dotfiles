Shemuel — here's what I'd build, what it costs, and what I won't touch.

## Where things fall through

Twelve schemes. Twelve mailboxes. Complaints, contractor quotes, trustee
approvals and payment chases all live in email — and email forgets.

Nobody can say what's still open, who has it, or how long it's been sitting
there. Meanwhile Zoho and JotForm cost you about **R3,800 a month** and nobody
really uses either one.

## What I'm not touching

**WeConnectU.** It does its job. I won't replace it, redesign it, or migrate
off it. This is about the mess around it, not the thing at the centre.

## One queue, three ways in

I'd build this on **Odoo** — an open-source business platform that's been
running companies for fifteen years. Helpdesk, contacts, documents, reporting:
all of it already exists. I host it, run it and shape it around Uys Prop. You
never deal with it as software, only as a service.

Everything lands in the same place, no matter how it arrives.

**1. Email.** Every scheme mailbox flows into one queue — the twelve you run
today, and the one you sign next month. Trustees,
owners and contractors keep emailing you exactly as they do today — no logins,
no training, no "please use the new system from Monday". Your replies go back
to them as ordinary email.

**2. Forms.** A public link per complaint type — geyser, electrical, leak,
common-property damage, whatever you want — asking the questions your staff
would otherwise have to chase. Unit number, photo, when it started, access
times. The form fills the ticket in properly, so nobody opens a complaint that
just says "water problem". This is what replaces JotForm.

**3. WhatsApp.** Later, and priced on its own. It's a real option, it needs a
message provider and a number, and I'd rather get email and forms
solid first than half-do three channels at once.

Once something is in the queue it carries a name and a clock: who has it, how
long it's been open, what was said. Nothing falls through.

## Where the owner data comes from

Your staff shouldn't have to open WeConnectU to find out who unit 14 belongs
to. So the unit and owner details sit right on the ticket.

- **Step 1:** I do it once, by hand, with a real export from one scheme — so
  you see your own data on your own tickets before you commit to anything.
- **Step 2:** the full load, all twelve schemes, with a repeatable refresh —
  scheduled, so an owner change reaches the helpdesk without anyone
  remembering to do it.
- **Always read-only.** Data flows out of WeConnectU, never back into it. It
  stays your system of record, and nothing I build can corrupt it.

Owner details are personal information, so the helpdesk is treated as POPIA
territory: only your staff can see it, and it's hosted in a place we agree on.

Add your whole team. Add the new admin next month. Sign three more schemes.
The monthly fee doesn't move.

Trustees, owners and contractors never appear on the bill at all — they're
outside the system, not seats in it. And this isn't an introductory offer:
