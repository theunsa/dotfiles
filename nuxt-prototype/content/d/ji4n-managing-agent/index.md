## The problem

Your team manages 12 schemes through 12 mailboxes. Complaints, contractor quotes,
trustee approvals and payment chases all live in email — and it's become too much
to track. Meanwhile you pay ~R3,800/month for Zoho and JotForm that nobody really uses.

WeConnectU works well for what it does. It stays. This is about everything *around* it.

## The approach

One helpdesk, built on **Odoo** (proven open-source business platform, huge ecosystem):

- All 12 scheme mailboxes flow into a single ticket queue — nothing falls through.
- Every ticket is assigned, tracked, and visible: who has it, how long it's been open.
- Trustees and contractors keep using plain email — no new logins for them.
- Unit and owner information from WeConnectU available right on the ticket.
- Replaces Zoho and JotForm — cancel both.

Built step by step, with your team involved at every step.
